    How it works

    From a registered song to a payment, in four steps.

    STEP 1

    Register

    Artists, managers, labels and publishers add works to the library, linked to their MCSN membership. Free for artists.

    STEP 2

    Install

    A local agent signs up the venue. The disc clips onto a magnetic plate screwed to the wall or ceiling.

    STEP 3

    Listen

    The device identifies registered tracks only and reports over its own SIM. A tamper alert and GPS show if it moves.

    STEP 4

    Bill and pay

    The venue pays one bill each month. MCSN pays rights holders using the measured plays.

    The device

    A disc that fits the room, not the other way round.

    White finish. All three colours come from the plastic itself, so every venue gets the same tested hardware.

    • Wall or ceilingOne body, magnetic plate, hidden lock
    • Own SIM and GPSReports on its own, shows if it is moved
    • Tamper awareAlerts on removal, cover and power loss
    • Registered music onlyEverything else is ignored

    Privacy by design

    It identifies songs. It does not record people.

    No conversations

    Speech is filtered out before anything is kept. Only a short music fingerprint is made.

    No raw audio stored

    The fingerprint is matched and discarded. Nothing is stored that could be played back.

    No customer data

    The log holds track, time and duration. Built to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

    The monthly music bill

    One bill. No surprise visits.

    The venue pays the MCSN licence fee and the PlayTrace service fee together, and gets a statement of what played. Licence fees go to MCSN. PlayTrace never sets them.

    For venues

    Cafes, bars, restaurants, salons, gyms, shops and hotels.

    If you play music for customers, the Copyright Act 2022 expects you to be licensed. PlayTrace makes that one monthly bill. Event venues are not covered.

    Zone agents

    Be the person your neighbourhood venues call.

    Love music? Own a zone of 10 or more venues, make sure every device is live, and make sure artists get paid. You report and escalate. You do not seal premises or collect licence cash.
